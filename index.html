The Story

Cutting hair since he was fifteen.

Jacob did his first cuts at fifteen. Friends at first, then friends of friends, then strangers who'd heard he was good. He never really stopped. He's thirty now, so he's been cutting hair for half his life.

A salon is also where he met his wife, years back. They have two kids now. Ask him about it and you'll get the whole story before your fade is done.

The thing fifteen years gives you is a good eye. Jacob will look at your face shape, how your hair grows, and what your week actually looks like, and give you an honest opinion. If the photo you brought in won't work on you, he'll say so. Then he'll tell you what will.

"Anyone can copy a picture. Knowing whether it'll actually look good on you, that's the job."
Jacob · JRT Styles
